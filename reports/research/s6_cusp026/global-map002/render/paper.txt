                     The Marked Attachment at a Toric Cusp

                                                       Raeez Lorgat


                                                           Abstract
           The toric specialization determines an actual marked map from the cusp boundary into its
       smooth neighborhood. Meridian mapping tori provide integral generators for its kernel in every
       degree. We compute the local relative homology from this map, including its degree-two and degree-
       three specialization. For two specified finite fillings, the first global attachment matrix is unimodular.
       The next homology group is expressed through the inclusions of two explicit meridian tori.
