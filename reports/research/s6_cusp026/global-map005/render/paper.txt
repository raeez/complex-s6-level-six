         The Third Homology of a Marked Cusp Attachment

                                                     Raeez Lorgat


                                                          Abstract
           Four meridian mapping tori determine the third homology of a specified two-filling manifold.
       Their connecting classes have one integral relation. A closed cochain on the actual attachment
       identifies this relation with a primitive core three-cycle. We compute the complete degree-three
       attaching matrix, including the two fibre classes. It is unimodular, and the resulting marked cusp
       attachment has zero integral third homology.
