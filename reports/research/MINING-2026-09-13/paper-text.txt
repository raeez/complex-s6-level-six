    The Level-Six Fibre Lattice and Two-Fibre Linking Forms
                                             Raeez Lorgat


                                                Abstract
          For the lattice Λ = H1 (F, Z) of a smooth complex two-torus fibre in Engel’s fibration, we
      give the two integral monodromy markings and determine the primitive invariant alternating
      form ξ, up to sign. The distinguished circle class δ0 and invariant covector ψ satisfy

                                               ιδ0 ξ = −6ψ.

     An explicit unimodular basis puts ξ in the form J ⊕ 6J; hence its discriminant group is
     (Z/6Z)2 , with a perfect alternating pairing and a six-dimensional irreducible finite Heisenberg
     representation. We also compute the complete Smith form of a two-fibre presentation,
     including the zero-determinant case, and identify its determinant with a denominator-cleared
     Seifert Euler number. For an explicitly constructed effective rank-one Seifert C∗ -bundle
     with two multiple fibres, we prove that the primitive integral Leray carrier is indexed by
     lcm(r, s) and has coefficient ∆/ gcd(r, s). The full global Seifert class also has a Z/ gcd(r, s)
     component which the coarse transgression does not see. We resolve the integral extension
     hidden by this differential and compute the symmetric torsion linking form of every effective
     two-fibre filling. For arbitrary integral local data, the doubled presentation has alternating
     elementary divisors (d, |∆|/d), where d = gcd(r, s, m, n). These pairings have specified,
     distinct carriers. Engel’s rank-four transgression remains a separate (3, 4) calculation.
