       Positive Meridians and Integral Chains of Torus Fillings

                                                  Raeez Lorgat


                                                     Abstract
            A cyclic change of disk coordinate turns a finite monodromy into a smooth central fibre. The
       generator that rotates the new disk positively acts on this fibre by the inverse of positive transport.
       We derive this inverse directly for an explicit Weierstrass family and retain the full integral period
       lattice of a four-torus. Two affine disk quotients then make the effect on boundary chains explicit.
       For each quotient we give its smooth disk trivialization, fibre covering, oriented boundary map, and
       finite integral chains in all degrees. The correctly directed transport determines a positive prism
       homotopy and the complete boundary projection. The affine descent is determined from the conor-
       mal coordinate of the origin section in the Weierstrass reduction. Its nonzero central translation and
       the winding of the original section combine in an explicit integral change of fibre coordinates. This
       gives the marked local filling and its positive boundary maps, under the stated smooth line-bundle
       reduction hypotheses. Distinct affine descents show why linear monodromy alone would not suffice.
