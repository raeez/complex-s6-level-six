\begin{abstract}
A degeneration of complex two-tori with two independent periods of
order one has a smooth filling from a periodic unimodular fan.
Its central surface is an angular quotient of a hexagon with opposite
edges identified. We construct the marked boundary map and its positive
monodromy, including the meridian of a specified section.
A finite cellular calculation proves integral surjectivity of
specialization in every degree. The dual image is exactly the integral
monodromy-invariant lattice. A smooth comparison retains arbitrary
holomorphic units in the periods and preserves the entire marking.
\end{abstract}
