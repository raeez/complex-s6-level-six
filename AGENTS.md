# Agent Rules

## Authority and safety

`AGENTS.md` is the canonical local contract. `CLAUDE.md` points to this contract.
Inherit the applicable safety and research rules from `~/ecosystem/INVARIANTS.md`.
Read its Git safety, worktree ownership, evidence, attribution, and mathematical-repair rules before making changes.
Use `~/ecosystem/AGENTS-HARNESS.md` for host-specific execution guidance when needed.
Load business and publication guidance only when the task concerns those domains.

Work within assigned files and preserve concurrent changes. Use an isolated worktree for non-trivial edits.
Research or local build authority does not authorize cross-repository integration, signing, sending, or publication.
Report proved results, unresolved obligations, and the next discriminating step with their evidence.
A bounded investigation can finish with an unresolved theorem. Never present that theorem as proved.

## Golden rule: no project management at any scale whatsoever in the manuscript

Every reader-facing component contains mathematics, never agents, prompts,
critiques, audits, workflows, tasks, commits, tests, progress, ownership, or
revision history. This applies to titles, sections, proofs, tables, captions,
footnotes, appendices, and metadata. State exact hypotheses and separate
theorems from conditional interpretations and open problems.

The archive and its extracted numerical claims are untrusted leads. Recompute
the lattice, monodromy, topology, and complex-geometric data from primary live
sources. Do not infer a VOA, QFT, or exceptional symmetry from level-six
numerology. Use Witten's 1980s problem-first exposition structurally, without
imitating wording. Pass these rules to every subagent.

## Repository-specific proof boundary

The current lattice theorem is abstract. Applying it to an Engel threefold requires independent recovery and verification of the geometric source data.

## Required research exposition review

Before mathematical or physical writing, read
`~/ecosystem/policies/research-exposition-ledger.md` and apply its numbered
requirements to the complete target. This includes the preface, body,
proofs, examples, appendices, captions, and metadata.

Begin with the governing problem, prerequisites, and content to preserve.
Use explicit attack–heal cycles: test truth and scope, then the whole
composition, transitions, definitions, calculations, sentences, and words.
Repair every justified finding and reread the changed material in context.
Each new object must be motivated, defined before use, and needed at its
point of introduction. Preserve physical context and substantive mathematics.
Remove mannered prose and operational scaffolding. Inspect the final render.
Record findings, dispositions, source hashes, and unresolved obligations
outside all manuscript sources and reader archives. A pass count or a clean
build does not establish convergence or mathematical correctness.

Read the canonical ledger once per unchanged hash; do not substitute this
short routing block for it. If it is unavailable, report that source gap
and follow the available requirements without claiming full conformance.
